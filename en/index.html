FAQ

FAQ

How do I remove a background for free?
Upload an image, click Remove background. The AI runs locally and extracts the subject to a transparent PNG. Swap to white/black or keep transparent, then download. Free, no watermark.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. The AI runs locally in your browser (WebAssembly); images are never uploaded — good for ID photos and private pictures.
Why is the first run slow?
The first time, about 190 MB of model and runtime files are downloaded (then cached). It runs locally, so speed depends on your device; use Wi-Fi for the first run.
What if the cutout isn't perfect?
AI cutout works best on a clear subject with a simple background; hair, semi-transparent edges and busy backgrounds may have artifacts. A more prominent subject usually works better.
